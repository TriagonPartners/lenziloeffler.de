Nicolas Lenzi Loeffler.

Formula 3 Driver.

Formula 3 at Sixteen

Nicolas „Lenzi“ Loeffler, 16, competes in a Dallara F312 under number 91 across Formula 3 series in Austria, Italy and the FIA Central European Zone. In his debut season, he currently leads four championship standings in his class.

With the season entering its final stages, he has competed in five of ten race weekends, recording ten class wins, two outright podiums and fastest laps in more than half of his races — underlining his consistent pace alongside school in Munich.

From a Kart at Six to a Dallara at Eleven

Karting at six. First Dallara at eleven. First timed formula race in 2023, second in the Formula Predator final in 2024, first full formula season in 2025.

Every step added around fifty horsepower. Formula 3 was the next one — not a jump.

The Car, the Team, the Craft

Dallara F312 with Mercedes AMG power: 245 hp on 460 kilograms, sequential six-speed paddle shift, 260 km/h. The generation Verstappen raced at sixteen.

With Franz Wöss Racing since Monza, in the colours of the ADAC Junior Team Südbayern.

Where the Season Stands

Leading the Austria Formel Cup (179 pts), the TopJet F2000 Italian Trophy (158), the Austrian Racing Car Championship (136) and the FIA Central European Zone D2F3.

Next up: F4 Spanish Championship, F4 German Championship, Eurocup-3 — the route towards FIA Super Licence points.

Season 2026 — Four Championships, One Car

One car, one entry, four championships — every weekend scores in all of them. After five of ten weekends he leads all four.

Grids of up to thirty cars across several car generations. Ten class wins, two podiums in the outright field.

Monza — Nine Places on Sunday

A qualifying defect put him fifteenth on a grid of 29. Nine places gained in race one, eight in race two, both class wins taken from the back.

57 points, two fastest laps — and the mid-season switch to Franz Wöss Racing. His strongest weekend of the year.

Slovakia Ring — From the Pit Box to P3

Race one started from the pit lane. Eleven places later: third in the outright field, both class wins, fourth-fastest lap of the entire event.

50 points from the weekend. Across this run of the season: 34 positions gained in six races.

The Step After 2026

Three routes for 2027, all of them in conversation: F4 Spanish Championship with a €500,000 development prize fund, F4 German Championship returning on the DTM platform, and Eurocup-3.

Team talks are under way — among others with MP Motorsport and TC Racing.
